# Evidence claim register

## Supported now

The following statements are supported by committed reproducible artifacts:

- DisasterReady replayed 1,000 frozen official NWS alert records with 100% production-normalizer completion and zero uncaught crashes.
- The production classifier agreed with the explicit reviewed event taxonomy on all 1,000 replayed records.
- Every eligible supported active alert in that corpus received a deterministic reviewed action plan.
- The controlled failure matrix passed 148 of 148 scenarios across provider, network, storage, preference, shelter, AI fallback, large-payload, and offline cases.
- Demo and real activity have separate analytics modes and are covered by schema tests.

These results are engineering evidence from a frozen corpus and controlled adapters. They are not proof of production uptime or emergency outcomes.

## Not supported yet

Do not claim any of the following until the linked work is complete:

- 100 successful Gemini outputs with 100% critical-action preservation
- 30 to 50 completed usability sessions or a measured SUS score
- expert validation, endorsement, or implemented expert recommendations
- native device accessibility approval
- remote push delivery
- a public production demo URL
- production user counts, retention, or emergency impact

## Application wording

For college applications, describe what was built, tested, measured, and revised. Use the exact denominators above. Keep pending work pending. Do not imply that an external organization reviewed or endorsed DisasterReady unless written evidence exists.
