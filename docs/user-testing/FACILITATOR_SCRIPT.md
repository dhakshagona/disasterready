# DisasterReady facilitator script

## Opening

Thank you for helping test DisasterReady. This session uses simulated emergency information. It does not test you, and it does not replace official emergency instructions. If you believe there is a real emergency, stop and contact local authorities or 911.

Ask for recording consent separately. Participation can continue without recording.

## During tasks

- Read the scenario exactly as written in `docs/USER_TESTING.md`.
- Do not point at a control or explain a label unless the participant asks for help.
- Record what happened before recording why you think it happened.
- Ask neutral follow-ups such as, "What do you expect this to do?" and "What tells you whether this is real?"
- Stop immediately if the participant becomes distressed or believes the scenario is real.

## Closing questions

1. What felt most urgent on the active-alert Home screen?
2. What, if anything, made the Safety Route feel trustworthy or untrustworthy?
3. What did "verified" mean to you on the shelter result?
4. How did you distinguish AI wording from official alert text?
5. Was any text difficult to read or any action difficult to reach?

Remind the participant that no production behavior or safety outcome is being claimed from one session.
